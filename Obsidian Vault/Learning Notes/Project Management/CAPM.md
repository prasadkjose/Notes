# Project Life cycle and Processes

1. **Project** - Temp endeavor with a beginning and ending
	1. Goal and Deliverables
	2. Clear scope and objectives
	3. Specific Resources and Budgets
		1. Team, HW, SW, etc are resources
2. **Program** - 
	1. Group of related projects with inter dependencies and a common objective. 
	2. Longer Timeline and Larger scope.
3. **Portfolio** - Collection on projects, program and operations that align to a common **strategic** objectives. 
	1. Broad Scope
	2. Used to prioritize and manage projects and programs. 
4. **Project**(temporary) vs **Operations**(indefinite, like maintenance)
5. Predictive vs Adaptive(agile) projects: 
	1. Predictive(waterfall)
		1. Long term to predict future events and outcomes. 
		2. Detailed plans and budgets
		3. Rigid
	2. Adaptive(agile): 
		1. Uncertain future, flexible, short term. 
		2. May be less efficient.
6. **Project Management Life cycle - IPECC**
	1. Initiating
	2. Planning
	3. Executing
	4. Monitoring and controlling
	5. Closing
7. Risk- 
	1. Uncertain event or condition
	2. Positive or negative effect
	3. Identified, analyzed, documented
	4. Risk Register - DB of details of the risk
	5. Pure(physical) vs business
8. Issue
	1. Risk event that has occurred
	2. Issue Log, Owner, Resolution Date
	3. Example: Tech failures, Data security breach, Dependencies
9. Assumptions vs Constrains
	1. Assumptions may be risks. Believe but not proven to be true. 
	2. Constrains are limitations. It's true and are part of requirements. Budget, Deadline, Resources
10. Project Scope: 
	1. **Statement**: Before the project starts
		1. Goals and objectives
		2. Delierables
		3. Requirements
		4. Constraints
		5. Assumptions
		6. Exclusions - Used to clear up any assumptions. 
		7. High Level risks. 
	2. Review and Critique the statement. 
		1. Are objectives defined and scoped?
		2. Do objectives align with the goals of the org. 
		3. Are deliverables realistic. 
		4. Are requirements necessary and relevant. 
		5. Are constrains/exclusions reasonable and necessary. 
		6. Are costrains/assumptions defined and understood. 
11. Process Management Processes (49):
	1. Project **Integration** Management: 
		1. Charter
		2. Plan

		2. Defining

	4. Project **Cost** Management

	5. Project **Quality** Management:

		2. Managing

	6. Project **Resource** Management: 
		1. Planning

		6. Controlling 
	7. Project **Communication** Management

	8. Project **Risk** Management
		1. Planning

	9. Project **Procurement** Management.

	10. Project **Stakeholder** Management
